Questions

How is this different from Cloudflare?

Cloudflare's bot management is powerful but built and priced for enterprises. Bot Blocker is built for the other 99 percent of websites: small businesses that need protection without a security team or an enterprise contract.

Will it annoy my real customers with CAPTCHAs?

No. Legitimate visitors pass invisibly in nearly every case. Only suspicious traffic ever sees a challenge, and most bots are stopped before that point.

What about good bots, like search engines?

Search crawlers, uptime monitors, and other legitimate automation are allowlisted automatically. You can also approve specific bots with one click.

How much will it cost?

Pricing is not finalized, but the whole point is affordability for small businesses. Expect a free tier for small sites and simple flat monthly plans. Beta members lock in early pricing.
